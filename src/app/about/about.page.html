About About

Unfollowers is a small, single-purpose app: it tells you who stopped following you, and nothing else.

Your data stays here

Your export is unzipped and compared inside this app, on your device. Nothing is uploaded, there is no account to create, and no third party sees your follower list.

One thing is kept between visits: the handles of your followers from your last import. That is what lets the next import tell you who left. Forget my data on the home tab deletes it.

Getting your export

  1. Instagram → Settings → Accounts Centre → Your information and permissions.
  2. Export your information → Download to device.
  3. Select Followers and following, all time, format JSON.
  4. Instagram emails you a ZIP. Drop it onto the home tab.

Why an export, and not a login

Instagram retired the Basic Display API in December 2024, and the Graph API reports a follower count but never the list. There is no sanctioned way to read who follows an account, so the export is the only route that is both complete and permitted.
